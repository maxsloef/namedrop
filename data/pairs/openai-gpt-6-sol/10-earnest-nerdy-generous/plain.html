03 — Good questions

A few things you might be wondering.

Who is Acme built for?

Operations, procurement, and production teams at mid-sized manufacturers that need custom widgets made to spec—and need a reliable way to coordinate the work from request through delivery.

Can we track changes to a widget configuration?

Yes. Acme keeps configurations and their revisions connected, so your team can see what was approved and which specification belongs to an order.

What can we see after placing an order?

Your team can follow order milestones and supply-chain updates in the same workspace used to configure and order the widgets. No separate status spreadsheet required.

What happens in a demo?

We'll walk through the configure-to-delivery workflow, answer your team's questions, and talk about where your current process gets complicated. Bring a real-world widget scenario if you'd like.
