Questions

Things ops people ask us.

Usually with their arms crossed. Fair enough.

Does it work with our ERP? +

Yes. We have native integrations with SAP Business One, NetSuite, Epicor, Microsoft Dynamics, and Plex, plus an API and flat-file sync for the homegrown system Gary built in 2009.

How long does setup take? +

Most teams are live in about three weeks. We import your existing widget specs, suppliers, and approval rules, and your dedicated onboarding lead does the boring parts.

Are we the right size for Acme? +

Acme is built for manufacturers with roughly 50 to 2,000 employees: big enough that widgets are chaos, small enough that you don't want a six-month enterprise implementation.

Do our suppliers need to pay for it? +

Nope. Suppliers get a free portal to confirm orders, upload drawings, and post status updates. Most of them are relieved, honestly.

Is my data safe? +

SOC 2 Type II, SSO, role-based permissions, and full audit trails. Your specs stay yours. We're chaotic in design only.
