asked & answered

Questions, loudly answered.

Who is Acme built for?

Operations, procurement, and supply chain teams at manufacturers with roughly 1 to 30 plants who order custom or configured widgets from outside suppliers. If your team juggles specs, POs, and shipment status across multiple systems, you're our people.

Does it work with our ERP?

Yes. Acme ships with native connectors for SAP S/4HANA, NetSuite, Oracle, and Microsoft Dynamics, plus a REST API and flat-file sync for everything else. POs, receipts, and item masters stay in sync both ways.

Do our suppliers need to sign up?

No. Suppliers can receive orders by email, EDI, or their own portal, and update status through a no-login link. Those who want deeper visibility can join Acme free.

How long does implementation take?

Most customers are live in three to six weeks. Our onboarding team converts your existing spec sheets into configurable templates, so you don't have to rebuild your catalog by hand.

How is Acme priced?

Annual plans based on the number of plants you run, with unlimited users. We'll walk through exact numbers on the demo, based on what you actually need.
