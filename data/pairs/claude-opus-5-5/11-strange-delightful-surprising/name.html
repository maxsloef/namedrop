4

Six rules for a well-behaved factory.

What's inside the platform, arranged like a set of instructions for a toy: plainly, with pictures, and without anyone needing a certification to understand it.

I

A configurator that knows the rules.

Encode your engineering constraints once. Operators can only build widgets your suppliers can actually make.

II

Supplier routing by fit, not habit.

Acme scores every supplier on capacity, price, lead time and quality history, then recommends where each order should go.

III

Approvals without the reply-all.

Set thresholds by plant, cost center or part family. Approvers get one clear request and one clear button.

IV

ERP sync, both directions.

Native connectors for SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics and Epicor. POs out, receipts back, nothing retyped.

V

Tracking you can trust.

Supplier updates, carrier scans and QC results in one timeline per lot, with early warnings when anything drifts.

VI

Secure, sensibly.

SOC 2 Type II, SSO with Okta and Azure AD, role-based access per plant, and a full audit log of every change.
