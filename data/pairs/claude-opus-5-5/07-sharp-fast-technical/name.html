01The problem

Custom widgets move through six systems, four inboxes and one spreadsheet nobody trusts.

Spec drift

Drawings change. Orders don't.

Revision C goes to the supplier while engineering approved Revision D. You find out at incoming inspection.

Fragmented ordering

Every supplier is a new portal.

Buyers re-key the same configuration into email, supplier sites and the ERP, and pray the quantities match.

Blind shipments

"Where is it?" is a daily meeting.

Status lives in phone calls. Line stoppages are the first signal that a lot slipped two weeks ago.

02The platform

One record per widget, from first parameter to final receipt.

2.1

Configure

Build widgets from parametric templates your engineers define. Dimensions, materials, finishes and tolerances validate live against manufacturability rules, so impossible parts never reach a quote.

  • Rule-based validation
  • Revision locking
  • CAD & drawing attach
2.2

Order

Send one configuration to multiple qualified suppliers, compare quotes side by side, and push the approved PO straight to your ERP with the exact revision attached.

  • Multi-supplier RFQ
  • Approval chains
  • ERP PO sync
2.3

Track

Follow every lot through machining, finishing, QA, transit and receiving. Acme predicts ETAs from supplier history and flags risk days before it hits your line.

  • Lot-level status
  • Predictive ETAs
  • Exception alerts
2.4

Analyze

Supplier scorecards built from real delivery and quality data, not self-reported surveys. Know who ships on time, who ships to spec, and what each widget actually costs you.

  • On-time & in-spec rates
  • Landed cost
  • Exportable reports
