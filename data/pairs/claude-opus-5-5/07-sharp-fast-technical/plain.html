FAQ

Questions ops leaders ask first.

Do our suppliers have to buy anything? +

No. Suppliers get a free portal to receive specs, submit quotes, and post status updates. Larger suppliers can stay on EDI, and Acme maps it for you.

What if our widgets are genuinely one-off? +

That’s the point. Acme is built for configure-to-order and engineer-to-order parts. Rules handle the common constraints; anything outside them goes to engineering review with the full context attached.

How is Acme priced? +

Annual subscription based on the number of plants and active custom SKUs. Supplier seats and read-only users are always free. You’ll get an exact number on the demo.

Does it replace our ERP? +

No. Your ERP stays the system of record for financials and inventory. Acme handles the configuration, sourcing, and tracking work that ERPs are bad at, and syncs the results back.

Who does the implementation? +

Our own solutions engineers, most of whom came from manufacturing ops. A typical rollout takes about six weeks and needs roughly four hours a week from your team.
