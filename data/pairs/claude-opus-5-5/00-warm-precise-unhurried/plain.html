How Acme works

Three steps, done properly, every time.

Acme follows the way your team already works. We simply keep the spec, the order and the shipment attached to one another, so nothing drifts.

Step 01Configure

Configure

Build each widget from your approved materials, tolerances and finishes. Acme checks every choice against supplier capability before anyone hits send.

  • Guided spec builder with rule checks
  • Revision history with named approvers
  • Import from CAD, PDF or existing part numbers
Step 02Order

Order

Compare quotes from your qualified suppliers side by side, then issue a purchase order with the exact spec attached. No retyping, no drift.

  • POs sync to your ERP automatically

Questions

The things people usually ask.

How long does rollout take?

Most single-plant teams are placing live orders within three weeks. Multi-plant rollouts typically take six to ten weeks, one plant at a time. A named implementation lead stays with you throughout.

Do our suppliers need to use Acme?

No. Suppliers can respond through a simple, free portal, or keep working by email and we’ll capture their updates. Larger suppliers often connect directly, but it’s never required.

Will this replace our ERP?

It won’t, and it shouldn’t. Acme handles the detailed work of custom widgets and syncs purchase orders, receipts and costs back to your ERP, which remains your system of record.

How is Acme priced?

Annual pricing based on the number of plants, with unlimited users and suppliers. We’ll share exact figures on the demo call. There’s no charge per order or per widget.

What about sensitive or export-controlled parts?

Acme is SOC 2 Type II certified and offers ITAR-ready workspaces hosted in US-only infrastructure, with role-based access down to individual drawings.
