The way it's been

A custom part shouldn't need a paper trail as long as a hallway.

Somewhere in your plant there's a spreadsheet named widgets_FINAL_v7. There's an email thread about thread pitch that went quiet in March. There's a supplier who swears the drawing they received said 12 millimeters.

Your team isn't disorganized. They're just working with tools that were never built for things made to order. Acme gathers the specs, the approvals, the purchase orders and the shipping updates onto one table, so everyone is looking at the same widget at the same time.

The platform · six pieces that fit together

Everything a widget needs, and nothing it doesn't.

Like a good set of building cards: each part is useful on its own, and they slot together into something sturdier than the sum.

01 · Configure

A configurator that knows the rules

Set dimensions, materials, finishes and tolerances with guardrails your engineers define once. Impossible combinations simply can't be ordered.

02 · Approve

Sign-offs without the chase

Route each configuration to engineering, quality and finance in the order you choose. Approvers get a clear summary and a single button.

03 · Source

Suppliers, side by side

Send one clean spec to several vetted suppliers and compare quotes, lead times and past on-time rates on a single, tidy page.

04 · Track

Every order, every mile

Live status from the supplier's floor to your receiving dock, with carrier updates pulled in automatically and delays flagged before they bite.

05 · Understand

Reports you'll actually read

Spend by plant, lead-time trends, supplier scorecards. Plain charts, plain language, and a Monday digest that fits on one screen.

06 · Connect

Plays well with your ERP

Two-way sync with SAP, NetSuite, Epicor, Plex and Infor. Part numbers, POs and receipts stay in step without anyone retyping a thing.
