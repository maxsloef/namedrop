01 — Approach

Good widget software is as little software as possible.

We removed every screen, field, and notification that didn't help a planner get the right part to the right line on time.
01

Useful

Built around the three things your team actually does: specify a widget, release an order, and confirm it arrived. Nothing competes with that.

02

Understandable

A new buyer can configure a stainless coupling and release a PO on their first morning. No certification courses, no consultants.

03

Honest

Prices, lead times, and supplier performance are shown as they are, including when a supplier is running late. Especially then.

04

Unobtrusive

Acme sits alongside your ERP and syncs POs both ways. Your planners keep their workflow; the paperwork simply disappears.

05

Thorough

Every revision, drawing, and inspection record is attached to the part number that produced it. Audit trails are built in, not bolted on.

06

Long-lasting

Customers from our first year are still here. Configurations saved in 2017 reorder in two clicks today, with the same revision history.
