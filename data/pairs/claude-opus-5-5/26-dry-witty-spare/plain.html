01 — How it works

Three steps. We checked whether it could be two. It could not.

Step 01

Configure

Specify material, tolerances, finish, and packaging against your approved spec library. Acme flags anything your supplier can't actually make before they tell you three weeks later.

Revision history included, for settling arguments.

Step 02

Order

Route purchase orders to the right supplier, with approvals that follow your rules rather than whoever is loudest. Quotes are compared side by side, like adults.

Integrates with SAP, NetSuite, and Oracle. Also Dave's spreadsheet, reluctantly.

Step 03

Track

Watch every widget from mill to dock: production status, shipping, customs, receipt. Delays surface on day one, not the morning the line stops.

You will know where your widgets are. Imagine.

04 — Questions

Reasonable questions, straight answers.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are placing orders within three weeks. We import your specs, suppliers, and open POs. You mostly watch.

Do our suppliers need to sign up?

No. They can use a free supplier portal, or keep replying to emails. Acme reads those too, and turns them into status updates.

What does it cost?

Annual plans priced by order volume, not seats. Add your whole team; we won't send a sternly worded invoice.

Is it secure?

SOC 2 Type II, SSO, role-based permissions, and full audit logs. Your widget specs are safer than they've ever been in that PDF.

We only make some custom widgets. Is that enough?

If you order more than a few hundred SKUs a year, yes. If you order four, we admire your restraint and suggest a notebook.

What happens in the demo?

We load one of your real widgets, order it, and track it. Thirty minutes. Questions encouraged; slides discouraged.
