§ 01 — Method

Three disciplines, practised the same way at every plant.

Custom widgets fail quietly: a revision that never reached the supplier, a purchase order raised twice, a pallet no one can place. Acme replaces the spreadsheets and email threads with a single, patient record.

§ 04 — The Record

Built to be kept, not merely used.

Every configuration, approval, order and movement is written to an append-only history. Years from now, you will still know why a part was made the way it was.
