§ 1 · Method

A method, not a marketplace. Three movements, kept in one place.

Most widget programmes live in a drawing folder, an ERP, a supplier inbox and somebody's spreadsheet. Acme replaces the gaps between them with a single, continuous record.

I.

Configure

Define a widget once, precisely. Material, geometry, tolerances, finish and compliance are held as structured specifications, with every revision signed, dated and preserved. Engineering approves; operations orders. Nobody works from an outdated drawing again.

  • Parametric families, not one-off PDFs
  • Revision history with named approvers
  • Material certificates attached at source
  • RoHS, REACH and PPAP fields built in
II.

Order

Issue purchase orders directly from an approved specification to suppliers you have already qualified. Quotes, lead times and pricing arrive in the same record, so the comparison is honest and the decision is documented.

  • Request quotes from up to twelve suppliers
  • POs written back to SAP, NetSuite or Epicor
  • Blanket orders and scheduled releases
  • Approval thresholds by plant and value
III.

Track

Follow each lot through casting, machining, inspection, freight and goods-in. When a supplier slips, Acme tells you what it affects on your line, how many days you have, and which alternate source can still make the date.

  • Milestones confirmed by suppliers, not guessed
  • Line-impact forecasting, 21 days ahead
  • Lot and heat-number traceability
  • Nonconformance logged against the unit
§ 2 · The Record

The provenance of a single widget.

Every part in Acme carries its own history: who specified it, who made it, what it was made from, where it has been, and what happened along the way. Nothing is overwritten. Nothing is lost when someone leaves.
