§02 · What Acme does

Three jobs. Done properly.

We don't do everything. We do custom widgets: the parts that are specific to you, sourced from a handful of suppliers, and fatal when they're late.

01

Configure

Build each widget spec once, from a parametric template. Engineering approves it. Revisions are locked, versioned, and impossible to send by accident.

  • One source of truth per part number
  • Approval chains your QA lead actually signs
  • Imports from SolidWorks, Onshape, and your old PDFs
02

Order

Send a locked spec to the right supplier in two clicks. Acme compares their real delivery history, not their sales rep's promises, and keeps a backup queued.

  • Suppliers get a portal, not an email chain
  • POs sync to SAP, NetSuite, Epicor, or Plex
  • Price and lead-time history on every quote
03

Track

Every order, every stage, updated by the supplier and carrier, not by your planner. When something slips, you hear about it days early, with a fix attached.

  • Early warnings tied to your production schedule
  • The floor can check status without calling you
  • Supplier scorecards built from what actually happened
§03 · What we're not

Things other vendors won't tell you.

You've sat through enough demos that promised everything. Here's what Acme doesn't do, so you can rule us out fast if it matters.

✕

We are not an ERP.

We don't replace SAP or NetSuite. We plug into them and handle the custom-widget mess they were never built for.

✕

Setup takes 3–5 weeks. Not "minutes."

Migrating your specs and onboarding suppliers is real work. We do most of it. You'll still need a few hours from engineering.

✕

You might not need us.

If you order fewer than ~200 custom widgets a year from fewer than 3 suppliers, a good spreadsheet is fine. We'll tell you that on the call.

✕

Suppliers have to log in.

Most do within two weeks. A few old-school shops never will. We handle those by email parsing, and it's about 85% as good.

✕

No native mobile app.

The web app works on a phone on the shop floor. It's fine. It's not beautiful. That's on the roadmap for Q3.

✕

We won't fix bad suppliers.

We'll show you, with data, exactly which ones are bad. What you do with that is up to you.
