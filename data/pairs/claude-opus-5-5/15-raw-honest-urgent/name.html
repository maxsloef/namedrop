Nº 04 / The platform

Three jobs.
Done properly.

Step 01

Configure

Build the widget once, correctly. Dimensions, material, finish, and tolerances go into a structured spec that suppliers can't misread.

  • Rules-based configurator blocks impossible specs
  • Every revision versioned and locked to the PO
  • Engineering signs off inside Acme, not over email
Step 02

Order

Send one spec to three suppliers, compare quotes side by side, and place the PO in minutes. Approvals route themselves.

  • Side-by-side quotes on price, lead time, and history
  • Approval chains that match your org chart
  • POs sync straight back to your ERP
Step 03

Track

Every order, every milestone, every plant on one board. When a supplier slips, you hear about it days before the truck doesn't arrive.

  • Supplier milestone updates without phone calls
  • Slip alerts the moment an ETA moves
  • Lead-time scorecards for every vendor
