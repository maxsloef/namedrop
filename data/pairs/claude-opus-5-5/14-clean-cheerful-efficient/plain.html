FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Something else on your mind? Ask it on the demo, or email hello@acme.example.

Does Acme replace our ERP?

No. Acme sits alongside it. Your ERP stays the system of record for inventory and finance; Acme handles the messy middle of configuring, quoting, approving, and tracking custom widgets, then syncs POs and receipts back automatically.

Do our suppliers have to pay or install anything?

Never. Suppliers get a free browser-based portal to receive specs, submit quotes, and post production updates. Suppliers who prefer email or EDI can keep using it, and Acme reads those updates for you.

How long does implementation really take?

Most teams place their first live order within 30 days. A dedicated onboarding engineer handles the ERP connection and catalog import; your team typically spends about six hours total on setup.

How is our data secured?

Acme is SOC 2 Type II audited, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and supports SSO with role-based permissions. Suppliers only ever see the drawings and orders you share with them.

What does it cost?

Acme is priced per plant on an annual plan, with unlimited users and suppliers. We’ll give you an exact number on the demo. Most customers cover the cost with expedite savings in the first two quarters.
