How it works

Three steps. One source of truth.

No more spreadsheets passed between engineering, purchasing, and the plant floor. Everyone works from the same spec, the same order, the same status.

Step 01

Configure

Build widgets from approved parameters — material, tolerance, finish, thread — and Acme blocks combinations your suppliers can't make.

  • Rule-based configurator
  • Reusable spec templates
  • Engineering sign-off built in
Step 02

Order

Send the spec straight to the right supplier with pricing, quantities, and delivery windows attached. Approvals route themselves.

  • Multi-supplier quoting
  • Budget-aware approval chains
  • Purchase orders synced to ERP
Step 03

Track

Follow every order through production, inspection, and transit. Get a heads-up before a delay reaches your line, not after.

  • Live milestone updates
  • Delay alerts by plant
  • Receiving checklists on the dock

The platform

Everything ops needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Acme is built for teams of five to fifty who manage thousands of custom SKUs without a dedicated procurement IT department.

Spec library

Every approved widget, versioned and searchable. Reorder a part from 2019 in two clicks.

ERP sync

Native connectors for SAP Business One, NetSuite, Epicor, and Microsoft Dynamics.

Delay alerts

Get notified the moment a supplier slips a milestone, with the lines it affects.

Supplier portal

Suppliers update status and upload certificates themselves. No logins for you to manage.

Spend analytics

See cost per widget, supplier on-time rate, and lead-time trends by plant.

Approvals & audit

Role-based permissions and a full audit trail for ISO 9001 and customer audits.

Dock receiving

Tablet-friendly checklists to count, inspect, and accept deliveries at the dock.

Multi-plant

Run every site from one account, with plant-level views for local teams.
