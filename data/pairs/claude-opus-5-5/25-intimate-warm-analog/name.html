Why we built this

A short note before the software.

Dear operations team,

We know how custom widgets actually get ordered. There's a spreadsheet called WIDGETS_FINAL_v7, a phone call to a supplier who's out at lunch, and a sticky note on the side of the MRP screen that only Denise can read.

It works, mostly, until a line stops because a 60-millimeter hex part is sitting in the wrong plant, and three good people spend a Tuesday afternoon finding it.

We spent eleven years on factory floors before we wrote a line of code. Acme is the tool we wished someone had handed us: a place where a widget is described once, ordered from anyone, and never quietly disappears.

Maren & Theo Co-founders, formerly plant managers at two very noisy factories
How it works

Three simple parts that fit together nicely.

Like a good set of cards, each piece stands on its own, but slot them together and the whole supply chain holds its shape.

No. 01 / Configure

Describe it once.

Build every custom widget from a shared library of shapes, tolerances and finishes. Engineering signs off, and the spec never drifts again.

  • Versioned specs with plain-English change notes
  • Drawings, CAD files and approvals in one place
  • Reusable templates for your most-ordered parts
No. 02 / Order

Send it everywhere at once.

Split one order across three suppliers, compare quotes side by side, and release purchase orders straight into your ERP without retyping a thing.

  • Quotes from every approved supplier in one view
  • Native sync with SAP, NetSuite, Epicor and Plex
  • Approval rules that match how your plant works
No. 03 / Track

Know where it is without asking.

Every widget carries its own story, from the supplier's floor to the truck to the exact bin on line four. Late parts raise their hand days early.

  • Live status from 1,200+ connected suppliers
  • Early warnings when a shipment will miss a build
  • Bin-level visibility across every plant
