A spreadsheet, three inboxes, and the one person who knows where everything is.

How we track widgets now:

  • "FINAL_v7_USE_THIS.xlsx"

Most mid-sized plants run on a quiet kind of heroism. A planner who remembers which supplier handles the tight tolerances. A buyer who knows to call on Tuesdays. A spreadsheet that works — until the week it doesn't.

Acme doesn't replace those people. It writes down what they know, so it lives in one shared place instead of in someone's head. Specs, quotes, purchase orders and shipment status sit side by side, and everyone on the team sees the same page.

When Dave goes on vacation, the widgets keep moving.

02 — HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, done the same way every time.

No new vocabulary to learn. Acme follows the way your team already works — it just keeps better notes.

Configure

Step 1

Build a spec from your own library of parts, materials and drawings. Tolerances and revisions travel with the part, so nobody orders from last year's print.

  • Reusable part templates
  • Drawing & revision history
  • Approval before anything ships

Order

Step 2

Send the spec to your approved suppliers in one go. Quotes come back into Acme, side by side, and the PO goes out with a click — already matched to the right part.

  • Supplier portal, no logins needed
  • Quote comparison at a glance
  • Syncs with your ERP

Track

Step 3

Every widget carries a status you can actually trust — updated by the supplier, the carrier and your own receiving team. Late orders raise their hand early, not the day they're due.

  • Live status from floor to dock
  • Early warnings on slipping dates
  • Receiving & QC sign-off

07 — FAIR QUESTIONS

Things people ask us before they book.

How long does it take to get set up?

Most plants are placing real orders in Acme within three weeks. We import your part library and supplier list for you, and a named onboarding lead stays with you through your first fifty orders.

Do our suppliers need to learn new software?

No. Suppliers get a simple link to confirm quotes and update status — no account, no training. Most of them tell us it's easier than the emails.

Does it work with our ERP?

Acme syncs with Epicor, Plex, NetSuite, SAP Business One and Infor SyteLine out of the box, and has an open API for anything homegrown.

What does it cost?

Pricing is per plant, not per seat, so everyone who touches a widget can use it. We'll give you a real number on the demo call — no "contact sales" runaround.
