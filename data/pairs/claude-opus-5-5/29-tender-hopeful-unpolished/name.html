Here is how it goes.

Three things happen to every widget. It is imagined, it is asked for, and it travels. Acme gives each of those moments a home, and lets the people on your team see the same picture at the same time.

Configure

Choose the size, the thread, the finish, the tolerance. Every option you've been emailing back and forth about lives in one patient place, with rules that catch the mistakes before they're machined. It remembers what you chose last spring.

Order

Send it to the right supplier for the right plant. Approvals happen where people already are, in the inbox or on the phone. Pricing and lead times sit right beside the part.

(No one retypes a part number ever again. Retyping is where the sorrow starts.)

Track

Watch it leave the machine shop, cross three states, and arrive at Dock 4. If it is going to be late, you'll know first, and you'll know why, with a day or two to do something about it.
