Questions, answered

Before you book

Who is Acme built for?

Operations, procurement, and supply chain teams at manufacturers with roughly 200 to 5,000 employees who order custom or configurable widgets from multiple suppliers. If your team juggles part variants, approval chains, and supplier updates across several tools, you’re exactly who we built this for.

Do our suppliers need to pay or install anything?

No. Suppliers get a free, browser-based portal and can also reply by email or EDI. Most confirm their first order within a day of being invited, without any training.

How long does implementation take?

Most customers are placing live orders within six weeks. A dedicated onboarding lead handles data migration, ERP connection, and supplier invitations, so your team can keep running the plant.

How is Acme priced?

Annual plans are based on the number of plants and internal users, with unlimited supplier seats included. We’ll walk through a tailored quote during your demo, and there are never per-order fees.

Is our data secure?

Yes. Acme is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data in transit and at rest, supports SSO via SAML, and offers role-based permissions down to individual part families. Drawings are only ever visible to the suppliers you choose.
