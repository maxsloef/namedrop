Questions

Good things to ask before a demo.

If yours isn't here, bring it to the call. We'd rather answer it honestly now than surprise you later.

Who is Acme built for?

Operations, purchasing, and planning teams at manufacturers with roughly 50 to 2,000 employees who order custom or configured widgets from outside suppliers. Most customers run between one and eight plants.

Does it work with our ERP?

Acme has native, two-way integrations with SAP Business One, SAP S/4HANA, NetSuite, Epicor Kinetic, Plex, and Infor SyteLine. For other systems, we use a flat-file or API connection, which our team sets up during onboarding.

Do our suppliers have to pay or install anything?

No. Suppliers get a free, browser-based portal to accept orders and post milestone updates. Suppliers who prefer email can reply to structured updates and Acme reads them in.

How is Acme priced?

Annual subscription based on the number of plants and active users, with onboarding included. Most mid-sized customers recover the cost within two quarters through reduced expedite fees alone. We'll share exact pricing on the demo.

What happens on the demo?

Thirty minutes with a product specialist who has worked in manufacturing operations. We'll walk through a widget order using parts similar to yours, answer questions, and outline what onboarding would look like for your plants. No pressure to decide anything.
