Configure. Order. Track.

Three operations, one record
Method

Every widget in Acme begins as structured data and stays that way. The configuration you define is the same record your supplier quotes against, your carrier scans, and your receiving team signs off. Nothing is retyped. Nothing drifts.

01.aParameter matrix

Configure

Define widgets as parameters, not drawings attached to emails. Dimensions, materials, tolerances, and finish are captured as structured fields and validated against each supplier's real capability before a single request is sent.

  • Parameters per widgetup to 480
  • Validation time< 400 ms
  • Version historyimmutable
01.bOrder allocation

Order

Route each configuration to the right supplier automatically. Acme weighs capacity, unit cost, and lead time across your approved vendors, then issues purchase orders your ERP already understands.

  • Approved vendors comparedall, every order
  • PO formatsEDI 850 · API · PDF
  • Approval rulesby value, plant, SKU
01.cUnit trajectory

Track

Follow every unit from raw stock to your receiving dock. Event-level telemetry from suppliers, carriers, and your own floor is reconciled into one timeline, with deviations flagged the moment they occur.

  • Event latency< 90 s
  • Granularitylot · pallet · unit
  • Exception alertsemail · Slack · Teams
