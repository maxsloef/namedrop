The platform

Three verbs. One place to do all of them.

Most ops teams juggle a CAD viewer, four supplier portals, a shared inbox, and a spreadsheet named FINAL_v7. Acme replaces all of it.

No. 01

Configure

Spec any widget — shape, material, thread, finish, tolerance — in a visual builder that knows what your suppliers can actually make. Impossible combinations get flagged before they become expensive ones.

  • Visual spec builder
  • Saved part families
  • Tolerance checks
  • Engineering sign‑off
  • Revision history
  • CAD import (STEP, IGES)
No. 02

Order

Send one spec to every qualified supplier at once. Compare quotes, lead times, and on‑time history side by side, then place the PO in one click — routed through the approvals you already have.

  • Multi‑supplier RFQs
  • Quote comparison
  • Approval routing
  • ERP sync
  • Blanket orders
  • Reorder triggers
No. 03

Track

Every widget, every stage, every plant — on one live board. When a batch slips at the mill, you hear about it that hour, with the three orders it affects and a suggested fix already attached.

  • Live supply board
  • Slip alerts
  • Carrier tracking
  • Dock scheduling
  • QA hold visibility
  • Supplier scorecards
