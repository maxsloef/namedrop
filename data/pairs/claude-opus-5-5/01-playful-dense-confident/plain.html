05 FAQ

Questions ops people actually ask us.

Do we have to switch suppliers? +

No. Bring your existing suppliers and we'll onboard them for free, usually in under a day each. If you want more options, you can tap into Acme's network of 1,400+ vetted widget makers, but that's entirely up to you.

How long does implementation take? +

Median is three weeks from contract to first live order. We import your spec library, connect your ERP and train your planners. You'll have a dedicated onboarding engineer who has worked a plant floor, not just a slide deck.

Will it work with our ERP? +

We have native two-way connectors for SAP, NetSuite, Epicor, Plex, Infor and Microsoft Dynamics 365, plus a documented API for anything homegrown. Your ERP stays the system of record.

We only have 2 plants. Is this overkill? +

Our sweet spot is manufacturers with 1 to 15 sites and $50M to $1B in revenue. Plenty of single-plant customers use Acme purely to stop late widgets from stopping the line.

How is Acme priced? +

A flat annual platform fee based on the number of plants, with unlimited users and suppliers. No per-order fees and no percentage of spend. We'll give you an exact number on the demo call.

What happens on the demo? +

Twenty minutes, no slides. Send us one real widget drawing beforehand and we'll configure it live, show you real supplier quotes and walk through how tracking would look for your plants.
