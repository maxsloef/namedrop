The quiet problem

Your supply chain runs on spreadsheets and goodwill.

A single custom widget touches an engineer, a buyer, three suppliers, a freight broker, and a receiving clerk before it ever reaches the line. Each of them keeps their own version of the truth — in an inbox, a shared drive, or their head.

So specs drift. Quotes expire. A revision gets lost between email threads. And the first sign of trouble is a stopped line and a very long phone call.
