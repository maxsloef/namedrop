The problem

Somewhere between the purchase order and the loading dock, your widgets go dark.

Specs live in one spreadsheet, supplier confirmations in someone's inbox, shipment updates in a carrier portal nobody checks. A tolerance change never reaches the vendor. A heat-treat delay surfaces the morning the line needs the part.

Most operations teams find out an order is late when production stops. It doesn't have to work that way.

Acme turns the lights on.

The platform

Three movements.
One continuous shot.

From the moment an engineer defines a widget to the moment receiving signs for it, Acme keeps the entire order in frame: one record, one owner, no cuts.

I. Configure

Specify it once.
Exactly.

Build custom widgets from your approved parameters: material, tolerance, finish, thread pitch, packaging. Acme checks every configuration against what each supplier can actually make before it leaves your desk, so engineering intent survives the trip to the vendor.

  • Versioned specs with complete revision history
  • Automatic supplier capability matching
  • Approval routing for engineering and quality
II. Order

The right supplier.
The right terms.

Send a configured widget to one supplier or split it across several. Acme compares lead times, price breaks, and each vendor's real on-time history, then routes the purchase order through your approval chain in minutes instead of days.

  • Multi-supplier splits and blanket orders
  • Lead-time and landed-cost comparison side by side
  • Two-way sync with SAP, NetSuite, Epicor, and Dynamics
III. Track

Know where every unit is.
Before anyone asks.

Follow widgets from production run to receiving dock across every plant you operate. Acme pulls status from suppliers and carriers, scores delay risk against your production schedule, and tells you which line will feel it while there's still time to act.

  • Live status across suppliers, carriers, and plants
  • Delay risk scored against your production schedule
  • Receiving and quality checkpoints at every dock

The whole floor.
