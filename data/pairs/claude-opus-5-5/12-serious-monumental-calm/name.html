The problem

A custom widget passes through eleven hands before it reaches your line. Most teams follow it with spreadsheets, email threads, and memory.

01

Specifications drift.

A tolerance changes in engineering. Purchasing orders from last quarter's drawing. The supplier builds exactly what they were told.

02

Orders scatter.

Four suppliers, three portals, one ERP that doesn't know the difference between a bracket and a flanged coupler.

03

Status is a phone call.

Nobody knows where the widgets are until the line stops. Then everyone knows, and everyone pays for expedited freight.

The platform

Three functions.
The same box, a different interior.

Acme is one system with one record of each widget. Configuration, ordering, and tracking are not separate tools stitched together — they are three views of the same object.

01 / ConfigureDiagonal plane

Configure

Build every widget from a governed specification: dimensions, material, finish, tolerance, and compliance. Engineering approves once. Everyone orders from the same revision.

  • Parametric widget templatesRev-locked
  • Tolerance and material rulesISO 2768
  • Drawing and CAD attachmentSTEP · DXF · PDF
02 / OrderHorizontal shelf

Order

Send approved specifications to qualified suppliers, compare quotes side by side, and issue purchase orders that write straight back to your ERP. No retyping, no drift.

  • Multi-supplier RFQ and awardSealed or open
  • Blanket and release ordersMin / max
  • Approval chains by spendConfigurable
03 / TrackVertical divider

Track

Follow each lot from supplier floor to your receiving dock. Acme reads supplier updates, carrier scans, and ASN data, and tells you before a late widget becomes a stopped line.

  • Lot-level milestone trackingReal time
  • At-risk alerts by line date72 h ahead
  • Receiving and inspectionScan to close
