The problem we exist to solve

Custom widgets hold your lines together. Yet most plants still manage them with spreadsheets, email threads and hope.

Specs drift between revisions. Orders go out with the wrong tolerance. A shipment disappears somewhere between the finisher and your dock, and a line stops. Acme replaces that uncertainty with one record of truth that every buyer, engineer and supplier works from.

I.
Configure

Specify once. Build it right every time.

Define widget families with your real constraints: materials, tolerances, finishes, thread standards. Acme guides every configuration through rules your engineers set, so an impossible part can never reach a supplier.

  • Rule-based configurator with engineering sign-off built in
  • Full revision history and side-by-side spec comparison
  • CAD and drawing attachments pinned to each revision
Fig. 02: Configuration record
II.
Order

Purchase with the whole picture in view.

Compare qualified suppliers on lead time, landed cost and historical quality before you commit. Approvals route automatically to the right people, and every purchase order carries the exact approved spec.

  • Supplier scorecards drawn from your own delivery history
  • Approval workflows that follow your spend thresholds
  • Two-way sync with SAP, Oracle, NetSuite and Epicor
Fig. 03: Weighted supplier score
III.
Track

Know where it is before anyone asks.

Every unit, lot and shipment is traced through machining, finishing, inspection and freight. When a date slips, Acme tells you days ahead, with the downstream work orders it affects already identified.

  • Stage-level visibility from supplier systems and carrier feeds
  • Early-warning alerts ranked by production impact
  • Lot traceability for audits and recalls in seconds
Fig. 04: Exception radar
