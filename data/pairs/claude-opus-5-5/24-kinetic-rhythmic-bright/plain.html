How it works

Three steps. One beat your whole team can follow.

Engineering, purchasing and the plant floor finally work from the same record. Every widget carries its spec, its PO and its location with it.

01 · Configure

Spec it once.

Build custom widgets from approved materials, tolerances and finishes. Acme flags anything your suppliers can't make before it ever becomes a PO.

  • Reusable spec templates
  • Engineering sign-off built in
  • Revision history on every part
02 · Order

Order in minutes.

Turn an approved spec into a purchase order routed to the right supplier, at contracted pricing, with volume breaks applied automatically.

  • Multi-supplier quotes side by side
  • Approval rules by spend and plant
  • Pushes straight to your ERP
03 · Track

See it land.

Follow each batch from supplier floor to your dock. When something slips, Acme tells you which lines it affects and what to do next.

  • Live status from 6,000+ carriers
  • Delay alerts before they bite
  • Receiving scans close the loop
