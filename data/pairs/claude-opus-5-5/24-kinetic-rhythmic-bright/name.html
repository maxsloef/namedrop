How it works

Three beats.
One record.

Most widget delays don't start on the shop floor. They start when a spec changes in one place and not another. Acme ties configuration, purchasing, and delivery to a single living record so every team moves on the same count.

01

Configure

Build the spec once. Materials, tolerances, finishes, and drawings live on one versioned record that engineering and purchasing both trust.

  • Guided configurator with rule checks
  • Redlines and approvals in context
  • Full revision history per widget
02

Order

Send RFQs to qualified suppliers, compare quotes side by side on price, lead time, and track record, then issue the PO without leaving the page.

  • One-click RFQ to approved vendors
  • Landed-cost quote comparison
  • POs pushed straight to your ERP
03

Track

Follow every lot from machining to receiving. Acme flags a slipping ship date days before it turns into a line stoppage.

  • Supplier milestone updates
  • Carrier tracking built in
  • Early-warning alerts by plant
