FAQ

Questions people actually ask us.

Collected from the last 300 demos. If yours isn't here, it's a good question to bring to one.

Do our suppliers need to pay for or install anything?

No. Suppliers get a free portal login to receive specs, submit quotes, and update order status. If a supplier refuses to use a portal (it happens), Acme can send specs by email and parse their replies — a person on our team reviews anything the parser isn't sure about.

We already have an ERP. Why wouldn't we just use it?

You should keep using it — Acme syncs to it. ERPs are excellent at transactions for known items. They're usually weak at the part before an item exists: configuring it, checking it's manufacturable, getting comparable quotes, and keeping revisions straight across teams. That gap is where Acme lives.

How long does implementation really take?

The median is 23 days from kickoff to first live PO. The fastest was 6 days (one plant, clean data). The slowest was 94 days (eleven plants, three ERPs, and a merger mid-project). We'll give you a realistic estimate on the demo based on your setup.

What happens to our data if we leave?

You can export everything — specs, revisions, quotes, POs, lot history, attachments — at any time, as CSV or Parquet, with no fee. After cancellation we keep a read-only copy for 90 days in case you need it, then delete it and send you a deletion certificate.

Is there a free trial?

Not a self-serve one, because an empty Acme account isn't very useful. Instead, the demo uses your real part, and if it looks promising we run a free 30-day pilot on one plant with your actual data and suppliers.
