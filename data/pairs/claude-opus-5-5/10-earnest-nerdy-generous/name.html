Six tools that slot together.

Use one, use all six. Each is useful on its own and more useful stacked with the others, like a good set of cards.

01 · Configurator

Configure

Build a widget from parameters, not paperwork. Acme flags impossible combinations — a 2 mm wall on a 90 mm bore, say — before a maker has to tell you.

02 · Sourcing

Order

Send one spec to several qualified makers and compare quotes on equal terms: unit price, tooling, lead time, and each maker's real on‑time history.

03 · Tracking

Track

Every order, down to the lot, from machining to inspection to your dock. Late shipments rise to the top automatically. Everything else stays politely quiet.

04 · Forecasting

Replenish

Connect your MES or ERP and Acme learns how fast each line consumes each widget, then suggests reorders with enough lead time to spare.

05 · Quality

Inspect

Attach CMM reports, first‑article inspections, and certs to the exact lot they describe. Auditors love it. So will your quality lead.

06 · Network

Connect

Invite your existing suppliers for free, or tap our network of 212 audited makers. We never mark up a quote and never sell your specs.

Fig. 4 — The journey of a single order

What happens after you click “order.”

A typical custom widget takes 18 days door to door. Here's where it spends them, and where Acme keeps an eye on it.

Day 0

Spec locked

Your configuration is versioned and signed off by whoever you choose. No more “which drawing did we send?”

Day 1–2

Quotes in

Makers respond inside Acme. Median time to three comparable quotes: 29 hours.

Day 3–14

Made

Makers post milestones: material received, first article, machining complete. You see them the moment they happen.

Day 15

Inspected

Inspection data attaches to the lot. Anything out of tolerance holds the shipment before it leaves the building.

Day 16–18

Received

Carrier tracking flows in, your dock scans it, and the bin is counted as full again.
