Why teams come to us

Custom parts shouldn't need a detective to track them down.

Most ops teams we meet are good at their jobs and tired of their tools. The spec lives in one inbox, the PO in another system, and the delivery date lives in someone's head. When a widget runs late, the line waits — and people spend their afternoon piecing together what happened.

Acme replaces all three with one shared record.

How it works

Configure. Order. Track.
That's the whole idea.

Acme follows each widget through its life, so the details you capture at the start are still there — intact and accurate — when it arrives at the dock.

STEP 01

Configure it once, properly

Build a widget spec from your approved templates — material, dimensions, finish, tolerances, drawings. Quality and engineering sign off right on the record.

  • Revision history you can actually follow
  • Rules that catch impossible specs early
STEP 02

Order with the right supplier

Send the approved spec to one supplier or request quotes from several. Pricing, lead times and confirmations come back into the same place.

  • Suppliers join free — no new logins to buy
  • POs sync to your ERP automatically
STEP 03

Track it to the dock

Watch progress as suppliers update it — production, inspection, shipping, receipt. If a date starts to drift, you'll hear about it days earlier, not the morning of.

  • Quiet alerts, only when something changes
  • Receiving checks against the original spec
Supply chain view

See your whole widget network, without the noise.

Every supplier, every open order and every shipment in motion — on one map your whole team can read at a glance. Planning, purchasing and the plant floor finally look at the same thing.

  • Early warnings on late ordersAcme learns each supplier's patterns and flags risk up to 9 days sooner.
  • Supplier scorecardsOn-time rate, quality holds and responsiveness — fair, factual, shareable.
  • Works with what you haveConnectors for SAP, NetSuite, Epicor, Plex and Microsoft Dynamics.

Questions

The things people usually ask us first.

Something else on your mind? Email hello@acme.example and a real person will reply within one business day.

How long does it take to get up and running?

Most teams place their first live order through Acme within three weeks. We import your existing widget specs and supplier list for you, and a named onboarding lead stays with you through your first 90 days.

Do our suppliers have to pay or learn new software?

No. Suppliers use Acme free, forever. They can update orders from a simple web link, by replying to an email, or through an API if they prefer. Most are set up in under ten minutes.

Will it work with our ERP?

Acme has native connectors for SAP S/4HANA, NetSuite, Epicor Kinetic, Plex and Microsoft Dynamics 365. For anything else, we offer a documented REST API and flat-file sync, and we'll scope it with you during the demo.

How is Acme priced?

Annual plans are based on the number of active plants, not per-seat — so you can invite everyone who touches a widget without doing math first. We'll share exact pricing on the demo call.

What about security and our drawings?

Acme is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data at rest and in transit, and supports SSO. Suppliers only ever see the drawings and specs on orders you've sent them, and every access is logged.
