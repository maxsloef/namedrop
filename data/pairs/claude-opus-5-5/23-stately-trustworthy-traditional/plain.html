Why Acme exists

Custom widgets deserve better than a patchwork of emails, PDFs and hope.

At most mid-sized manufacturers, a single custom widget order passes through a dozen hands. Engineering drafts a spec. Procurement re-keys it into a purchase order. A supplier returns a quote by email. Someone updates a shared spreadsheet — sometimes.

Each handoff is an opportunity for a wrong tolerance, a missed revision, or a shipment nobody knew was late. Acme replaces that fragile chain with one governed record, shared by your engineers, buyers, suppliers and plant managers alike.

The platform

Three disciplines, one dependable record.

Acme was built alongside operations teams who manage thousands of widget variants. It respects the way your business already works — and removes the parts that don't.

I.

Configure

Build widget specifications from approved templates, with rules that prevent incompatible materials, finishes and tolerances before they ever reach a supplier.

  • Rule-based configurator with engineering sign-off
  • Full revision history on every specification
  • CAD and drawing attachments kept with the spec
II.

Order

Issue purchase orders to your approved supplier panel, compare quotations side by side, and route approvals according to the spending authority you already have.

  • Multi-supplier quotation and award
  • Approval chains mapped to your delegation policy
  • Native sync with SAP, Oracle, NetSuite & Epicor
III.

Track

Follow every order through production, inspection, transit and receipt, across every plant and supplier — with early warning when a date begins to slip.

  • Milestone tracking with supplier confirmations
  • Exception alerts before delays reach the line
  • Receiving and quality records tied to each unit

    Common questions

    Answers for the careful buyer.

    Have a question not listed here? Our advisors are available weekdays from 8am to 6pm Eastern at 1 (800) 555-0142.

    Does Acme replace our ERP? +

    No. Acme sits alongside your ERP and handles what it does poorly: the configuration, quotation and tracking of custom widgets. Approved orders, costs and receipts flow back into SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Epicor or Microsoft Dynamics automatically.

    Do our suppliers need to pay for or install anything? +

    Never. Suppliers receive a secure portal at no cost, where they can respond to quotations, confirm dates and upload inspection records. Those who prefer email can reply as they always have, and Acme records the update.

    How is Acme priced? +

    Acme is licensed annually by plant, with unlimited users and suppliers included. Implementation is a fixed fee agreed in advance. Your advisor will prepare a written proposal following the demonstration.

    What happens to our data if we leave? +

    Your data remains yours. You may export every specification, order and record in standard formats at any time, and we provide a complete archive at the close of any agreement.

    Is Acme suited to a company of our size? +

    Acme is designed for manufacturers with roughly 200 to 5,000 employees, typically operating between two and twenty plants. It is thorough enough for regulated industries and straightforward enough to run without a dedicated IT team.
