The Method

One discipline, in three parts.

Spreadsheets, email threads and supplier portals each hold a fragment of the truth. Acme gathers them into one orderly practice.

I Configure

Specify once, correctly.

Build each widget from approved dimensions, materials and tolerances. Acme validates every combination against your engineering rules before it reaches a supplier, so impossible parts are never quoted, never cut, never returned.

II Order

Place it with confidence.

Route configured widgets to the right supplier with pricing, lead times and approvals in plain view. Purchase orders flow into your ERP without re-keying, and every revision is signed, dated and kept on the record.

III Track

Know where it stands.

Follow each widget from the supplier’s floor to your receiving dock. Acme reports production milestones, shipment status and exceptions as they happen, and tells the right person before a delay becomes a stoppage.
