Operations work should feel less like firefighting, and more like a clear morning.

Mid-sized manufacturers order thousands of custom widgets a year across dozens of suppliers. Acme holds every specification, every purchase order and every shipment in one steady view — so your team can stop chasing and start planning.

The platform

Six simple tools, arranged with care.

Each part of Acme does one thing well. Together they form a single, evenly-spaced grid for your entire widget supply chain.

i.

Configure

Build widget specs from approved materials, tolerances and finishes. Acme flags conflicts before they reach the factory floor.

ii.

Order

Send purchase orders to any supplier in two clicks. Pricing, lead times and minimums are compared side by side.

iii.

Track

Follow every batch from mill to dock with live status from your suppliers and carriers. No more “just checking in” emails.

iv.

Forecast

See demand three months ahead, drawn from your production schedule. Reorder points adjust themselves as plans change.

v.

Approve

Route spend and spec changes to the right people with clear, gentle approval flows that fit the way your plant already works.

vi.

Integrate

Connects to SAP, NetSuite, Oracle, Epicor and Microsoft Dynamics, so orders and inventory stay in sync with your ERP.

How it works

From a first sketch to a delivered widget, in five even steps.

01

Describe the widget

Choose a base design or start fresh. Set dimensions, materials and finishes from your approved library — Acme checks every value against your engineering rules.

02

Compare suppliers

Acme shows qualified suppliers with real quoted prices, capacity and on-time history, so choosing the right partner takes minutes, not days.

03

Place the order

Purchase orders are generated, approved and sent automatically, with the full spec attached. Your ERP is updated the moment it goes out.

04

Watch it move

Production milestones, quality checks and shipping updates appear on a single timeline. Delays surface early, while there’s still time to act.

05

Receive and learn

Receiving closes the loop. Every order refines your forecasts, supplier scores and costs — so the next one is a little easier.
