How it works

(it is simpler than you fear)

1

Configure it

Pick your materials, tolerances, finishes, and quantities in a builder that feels like arranging things on a kitchen table. Every spec is checked by a real engineer before it ever reaches the floor.

no 47-tab spreadsheets. we promise.
2

Order it

One order fans out gently across vetted suppliers in our network. Pricing is honest and shown up front; lead times are quoted by humans who will be held to them.

the truck is a metaphor. mostly.
3

Track it

Follow every widget from raw stock to loading dock on one calm timeline. Delays are flagged early, kindly, and with a suggested fix — never at 5 p.m. on a Friday.

you will always know where your widgets are.
