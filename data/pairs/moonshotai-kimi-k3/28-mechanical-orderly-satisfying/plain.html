Assembly Procedure

From spec to dock
in three steps.

No procurement black boxes. Every order moves through the same visible, auditable pipeline.

STEP 01

Draw it up

Pick a template or upload a drawing. Acme validates manufacturability on the spot and prices the run before you commit to anything.

STEP 02

Approve & route

Your approval chain signs off in-app. Acme assigns the order to a qualified supplier with open capacity and locks the delivery date.

STEP 03

Watch it arrive

Milestone updates flow into your dashboard and ERP. If anything slips, you know about it days early — with a recovery plan attached.
