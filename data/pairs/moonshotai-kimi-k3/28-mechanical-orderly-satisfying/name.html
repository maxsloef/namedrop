01 — Process

Three steps. No steps between steps.

A widget order should read like a well-made machine: few parts, each doing exactly one thing.

Step / 01

Configure

Specify dimensions, material, and finish in a guided spec sheet. Every option is validated against live machine capacity before you commit.

Step / 02

Order

One purchase order, one price, one delivery date. Acme routes your spec to the right line in our certified plant network — automatically.

Step / 03

Track

Follow each lot from first cut to final inspection with scan-level precision. Exceptions surface in hours, not at the loading dock.

03 — Platform

Less, but better software.

Four capabilities. Each one finished. Nothing decorative, nothing you will have to relearn next quarter.

A.1

Spec library

Every widget your company has ever ordered, versioned like an engineering drawing. Reorder a Rev C with one click — or branch it into a Rev D.

A.2

Live capacity pricing

Quotes are computed from real machine availability across the plant network, so the price and the date you see are the ones you get.

A.3

Lot-level tracking

Each production lot carries a scan history: cutting, machining, finishing, inspection. Share a read-only tracking link with anyone downstream.

A.4

ERP handshake

Native connectors for SAP, NetSuite, and Plex push confirmations, ASN data, and invoices back to your system of record. No swivel chair.
