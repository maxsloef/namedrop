The platform

Every step of the widget lifecycle, in one system of record.

Stop stitching together spreadsheets, email threads, and supplier portals. Acme replaces the patchwork with a single workflow your whole team can trust.

Guided configuration

Spec custom widgets with validated templates — tolerances, finishes, materials, and certifications enforced at entry, so bad orders never reach the floor.

Live order tracking

Follow every order from PO to dock with milestone updates from plants and suppliers. Exceptions surface early — not the week of the line run.

Supply-chain visibility

See capacity, lead times, and quality scores across your supplier network. Reallocate volume before a bottleneck becomes a missed shipment.

Approvals that move

Route quotes and change orders to the right approver automatically. Full audit trail included — no more hunting through inboxes for sign-off.

Quality built in

Inspection criteria travel with the order. QC results, certs, and non-conformance reports attach to each lot, ready for audit at any time.

ERP-friendly

Two-way sync with SAP, NetSuite, and Dynamics out of the box. Acme fits the stack you already run — no rip-and-replace required.

How it works

From spec to dock in three steps.

Configure

Build the widget spec once using guided templates. Acme validates every parameter against manufacturing rules and flags conflicts before you commit.

Order

Send the order to the right plant or supplier with one click. Pricing, lead time, and capacity are confirmed up front — approvals route themselves.

Track

Watch production progress in real time. Get notified on exceptions, receive QC documentation automatically, and reconcile on delivery.
