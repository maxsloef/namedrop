The platform

Everything your widget orders need, nothing they don't

We built Acme alongside plant managers and procurement leads, so every feature earns its place on the screen.

Guided configuration

Define dimensions, materials, tolerances, and finishes through a step-by-step builder that flags incompatible specs before they become costly mistakes.

Live order tracking

Follow every order from confirmed specs to loading dock. Milestones update in real time, and your team gets a gentle heads-up the moment anything slips.

Supplier visibility

See capacity, lead times, and quality history across your supplier network — so you can choose the right partner for each run, not just the nearest one.

One source of truth

Specs, revisions, quotes, and conversations live attached to the order itself. Anyone on the team can pick up exactly where a colleague left off.

Quality records, kept

Inspection results and certifications are filed automatically with each batch, so audits take minutes instead of a week of digging through inboxes.

Forecasting that helps

Reorder points and demand projections based on your actual consumption — quiet, useful numbers that keep the line running without over-ordering.

How it works

From spec to delivery in three steady steps

Configure your widget

Use the guided builder to set specs, or import an existing part number. Acme validates every combination against manufacturability rules before you commit.

Place the order

Compare quotes and lead times from vetted suppliers, then place the order with one click. Pricing is transparent — the number you see is the number you pay.

Track it to the dock

Watch progress through production, inspection, and freight. If a milestone moves, you know about it the same hour — with a revised ETA, not a surprise.
