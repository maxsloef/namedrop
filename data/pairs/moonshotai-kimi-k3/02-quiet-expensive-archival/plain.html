§ 01 — Position

Procurement should read like a record, not a scramble.

Most operations teams run custom parts through spreadsheets, inboxes, and memory. Acme replaces the scramble with a single, durable record — every configuration, every order, every shipment, kept in one place and always current.

i.

Configured, not improvised

Define widgets once — dimensions, alloys, tolerances, finishes — as governed specifications. Engineering changes propagate to every open order automatically, with a full revision history behind them.

ii.

Ordered, not chased

Quotes arrive against live capacity, not optimistic promises. Orders flow to vetted suppliers with confirmed dates, and pricing is recorded the day it's agreed — not reconstructed at invoice time.

iii.

Tracked, not wondered about

Every lot carries a live position: queued, in production, in inspection, in transit, received. Your team stops asking where things are, because the answer is already on the page.

§ 02 — Capabilities

Five instruments, one desk.

Everything an operations team touches between "we need a part" and "the part is on the shelf."

  • a.

    Specification Library

    A versioned catalog of every widget you make. Clone, revise, and retire specs without losing the lineage of what shipped when.

  • b.

    Guided Configuration

    Constraint-checked forms that make invalid combinations impossible at the source — before they become expensive on a factory floor.

  • c.

    Supplier Routing

    Match each order to qualified suppliers by capability, certification, and current load. Rebalancing takes minutes, not a renegotiation.

  • d.

    Lot-Level Tracking

    Milestones stream in from the floor and the carrier. Exceptions surface the hour they happen, with the person responsible already attached.

  • e.

    The Ledger

    Spend, lead time, and quality by supplier and by spec — exported clean to your ERP, or read straight from the page.
