Three principles, kept religiously.

Nº 01 — Principles
I.

Configure without counsel

Every widget begins as a quiet conversation between your specifications and our catalogue. Dimensions, tolerances, finishes, and quantities are set in minutes — pricing resolves instantly, with no calls and no quotes lost in inboxes.

II.

One ledger of record

Purchase orders, confirmations, and revisions live in a single archival ledger your whole team can read. What was ordered, by whom, and when — settled permanently, like entries in a well-kept diary.

III.

Follow every parcel

From foundry to loading dock, each widget carries its own thread of provenance. Milestones arrive as they happen, so your supply chain is something you observe — never something you chase.

Nº 02 — Method
01

Specify

Open the configurator and describe the widget as you would to a master machinist — material, geometry, finish, lot size. Feasibility and price settle in real time.

02

Commit

Route the order through your own approval chain inside Acme. Finance sees cost, operations sees lead time, and nothing leaves the building unsigned.

03

Follow

Watch production, quality inspection, and freight unfold on a single timeline. Delays announce themselves early, with remedies attached.

04

Archive

Every completed order is bound into your permanent record — reorderable in one touch, auditable forever. Institutional memory, kept for you.
