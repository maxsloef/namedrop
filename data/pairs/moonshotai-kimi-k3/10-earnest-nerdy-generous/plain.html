Fair questions

Asked by people like you.

We already have suppliers we like. Is this replacing them?+
No — bring them. About a third of our customers use Acme as the system of record for their existing supplier relationships, and tap our network only for overflow or new processes. Your suppliers get a clean portal instead of your email attachments. They tend to like that.
How big do we need to be for this to make sense?+
Our sweet spot is manufacturers with 100–2,000 employees spending at least ~$250k/year on custom components. Below that, our free tools are genuinely useful and we’ll say so on the demo call rather than sell you something premature.
What does implementation actually look like?+
Median time to first order is 9 days. We migrate your widget catalog and open POs ourselves (yes, a human does this, it’s included), connect your ERP, and train your team in two 45-minute sessions. No consultants, no six-month rollout.
How do you make money?+
A flat platform subscription based on order volume, published on our pricing page. We don’t mark up parts and we don’t take kickbacks from manufacturers — that would make our quotes suspect, and quotes are kind of the whole point.
Is our design data safe?+
SOC 2 Type II, SSO/SAML, region-pinned storage, and NDAs with every manufacturing partner in the network. Your drawings are shared only with the specific shops quoting your order, and we log every access. Happy to send the full security whitepaper.
