The Craft

Every widget, rendered in light and shadow.

Most procurement tools bury your operation in spreadsheets and guesswork. Acme carves away the noise, leaving only what matters — illuminated.

No. I

Configure Without Compromise

Define tolerances, alloys, finishes, and dimensions through a guided specification studio. Acme validates every parameter against live manufacturing capacity before you commit — so the widget you design is the widget that arrives.

No. II

Order Across the Chain

One purchase order fans out to vetted suppliers, contract manufacturers, and freight partners automatically. Pricing is negotiated in real time; lead times are committed, not estimated. You approve. Acme orchestrates.

No. III

Track to the Last Lumen

From furnace to loading dock, every widget lot carries a live thread of custody. Delayed heat treatment in week two? You know in hour one — with a revised arrival date and the cost impact already calculated.

The Method

Three movements, one composition.

I.

Compose

Import your drawings or build from our parametric library of 2,400 widget archetypes. Acme translates engineering intent into supplier-ready specifications in minutes, not weeks.

II.

Commission

Set quantity, deadline, and budget. Our sourcing engine stages competitive bids across your approved network and presents a single, recommended path — with alternates in reserve.

III.

Contemplate

Watch production unfold on one dark, quiet dashboard. Exceptions surface themselves. Everything else stays out of your way, exactly as it should.
