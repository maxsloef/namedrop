How it works

Three steps. No spreadsheets, no phone tag.

Ordering custom widgets used to mean faxed spec sheets and crossed fingers. Here's how it works now.

1

Configure

Pick your material, dimensions, tolerances, and finish from our catalog of 200+ widget families. Save spec sheets for your whole team, so everyone's ordering the same part — not three versions of it.

2

Order

Get an instant, honest quote with a real delivery date — not a range. Approve it, and your order goes straight to the right shop floor. Volume pricing and standing orders are built right in.

3

Track

Follow every batch from machining to quality check to your loading dock. If something slips, you hear it from us first — with a new date and a plan, not a shrug.
