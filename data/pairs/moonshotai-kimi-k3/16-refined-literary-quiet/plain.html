No. 1 — The Platform

Built for those who make things properly.

Three convictions shape every screen of Acme. They are simple, and we hold them firmly.

Precise configuration

Specify dimensions, tolerances, materials, and finishes in plain language. Acme translates your intent into manufacturing-ready specifications — no engineering drawings required.

Honest lead times

Every quote carries a delivery window drawn from live capacity across our supplier network. When something slips, you hear it from us first — with a revised date, not an apology.

End-to-end visibility

Follow each order from foundry to loading dock. One ledger of record for every widget, every revision, every shipment — shared with your whole team.

No. 2 — The Method

From specification to shelf, in four movements.

I.

Describe

Tell Acme what the widget must do. Our guided specification captures geometry, material, compliance requirements, and volume — in about the time it takes to write a memo.

II.

Compare

Receive firm quotes from vetted suppliers within one business day. Price, lead time, and quality history side by side, so the trade-offs are yours to weigh.

III.

Confirm

Place the order with a single approval. Acme locks the specification, issues the purchase documents, and opens a shared record your whole team can follow.

IV.

Follow

Watch production milestones, inspection results, and freight status as they happen. When the shipment arrives, the paperwork is already filed.
