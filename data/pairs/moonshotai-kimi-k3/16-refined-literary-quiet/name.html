Our Philosophy

In praise of fewer spreadsheets.

Somewhere between the purchase order and the loading dock, most manufacturing teams lose the thread. Specifications live in inboxes. Lead times live in someone's memory. The widget that arrives is almost — but not quite — the widget that was ordered.

We believe the tools of operations should be as considered as the products they help produce.

Acme replaces that quiet chaos with a single, deliberate system. Every custom widget — its dimensions, tolerances, finishes, and quantities — is configured once, ordered clearly, and followed patiently until it arrives. Nothing flashy. Nothing missing. Just the calm confidence of knowing exactly where everything is.

The Platform

One widget, understood completely.

Every order in Acme begins as a specification — a living document that travels with the widget from configuration to delivery. Your team, your suppliers, and your ledger all read from the same page.

  • Configure Guided specs with tolerances, materials & finishes
  • Order Approvals, POs & supplier routing in one flow
  • Track Live status from foundry to loading dock
  • Remember Every order archived, searchable, repeatable

How It Works

  1. Specify once.

    Build a widget from a library of materials, dimensions, and finishes. Acme validates every combination against what your suppliers can actually make — before you commit.

  2. Order without friction.

    Route approvals to the right people, generate purchase orders automatically, and send clean specifications to suppliers. No attachments. No version confusion.

  3. Follow it home.

    Milestones update as the widget moves through production and transit. Exceptions surface early, while there is still time to act on them.

  4. Reorder in a breath.

    Every specification is preserved. When stock runs low, a reorder is two clicks — the same widget, the same quality, the same quiet certainty.
