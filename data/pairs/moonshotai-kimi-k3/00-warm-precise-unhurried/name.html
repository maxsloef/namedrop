01 — The quiet cost of friction

Your line runs on widgets. Your week runs on email.

i.

Specifications scattered

Drawings live in one inbox, revisions in another, and the version on the shop floor is nobody's guess you'd like to make. Each ambiguity costs a call, a day, sometimes a batch.

ii.

Lead times you can't repeat

A quote that took nine days last quarter takes three this one — or the other way around. Without a system, every order is a first order, and planning becomes weather-watching.

iii.

Visibility that ends at the purchase order

Once the PO is sent, the widget disappears into a fog of "we'll check with the supplier." Your line, meanwhile, waits on a date you cannot defend.

02 — The method

Three movements. No noise between them.

Acme replaces the sprawl of inboxes and spreadsheets with a single, unhurried flow — from the part you imagine to the part in your hand.

Step one

Configure

Build your widget from governed templates — material, tolerance, finish, quantity — with drawings and revisions versioned in one place. What you specify is what gets made.

Step two

Order

Quotes return in hours, priced against live capacity. Approvals route themselves. One purchase order, one source of truth, lead times you can promise to a line manager without crossing your fingers.

Step three

Track

Every widget carries its story: machining status, inspection results, carrier, dock ETA. Exceptions surface before they become shortages. Your floor plans against fact, not hope.
