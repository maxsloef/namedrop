The Platform

Three pillars of a well-run supply chain

Everything your operations team needs to move a custom widget from specification to delivery — conducted with the rigor your business deserves.

— 01

Configure with precision

Guided specification sheets capture dimensions, materials, tolerances, and finishes exactly once. Every configuration is validated against manufacturability rules before it ever reaches a quote.

— 02

Order without friction

Approved quotes convert to purchase orders in a single step. Pricing, lead times, and terms are honored in writing — no renegotiation, no surprises buried in an inbox.

— 03

Track to the loading dock

Live milestone tracking follows each order from the mill to your receiving bay. Exceptions surface early, with a named account manager — not a ticket queue — on the other end.

How It Works

From drawing to delivery, in three measured steps

A process built on clarity. Most teams place their first tracked order within two weeks of onboarding.

I.

Specify your widget

Build the part in our configurator or import an existing spec sheet. Our engineers review every new configuration within one business day.

II.

Approve a firm quote

Receive binding pricing and a committed lead time within 48 hours. Volume tiers and blanket-order terms are presented plainly, up front.

III.

Watch it arrive

Follow production, quality inspection, and freight in one ledger. Your team is notified at each milestone — and immediately if anything shifts.
