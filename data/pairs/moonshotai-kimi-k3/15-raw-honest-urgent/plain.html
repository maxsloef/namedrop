The spreadsheet of doom
Fourteen tabs, three owners, one version called FINAL_v7_ACTUAL.xlsx. Nobody knows which numbers are real. Including you.

"It shipped." Did it?
Your supplier says the order left Tuesday. Tuesday of which month remains unclear. Your line can't run on vibes.

Spec drift
The widget that arrived is 2mm off from the widget you ordered. Somewhere between email #12 and email #31, the drawing changed. Nobody flagged it.

Reorder roulette

Firefighting as a strategy
