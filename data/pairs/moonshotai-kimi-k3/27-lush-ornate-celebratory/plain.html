The Platform

Three rooms in the grand house of Acme.

One platform, three crafts — each honed until ordering a custom widget feels less like paperwork and more like a ceremony.

Configure

Our guided spec builder translates engineering drawings into orderable widgets — tolerances, alloys, coatings, and all. Reuse saved configurations across plants with one click, and let built-in validation catch errors before they cost you a production run.

Order

Consolidate demand across facilities, compare live quotes from vetted widget foundries, and place multi-plant orders in a single flow. Approvals route themselves, budgets enforce themselves, and purchasing finally gets its evenings back.

Track

Follow every widget from molten alloy to loading dock on a live supply-chain map. Predictive ETAs flag at-risk shipments weeks early, and exception alerts land in your inbox — not buried in a carrier portal you'll never open.

How It Works

From spec sheet to dock door in three movements.

01

Import your specs

Drop in your drawings, BOMs, or legacy spreadsheets. Acme's parser builds a living catalog of every widget your operation depends on — organized by plant, line, and vendor.

02

Order with confidence

Set reorder points, bundle demand across sites, and let competitive quoting do its quiet work. Most teams place their first consolidated order within a single afternoon.

03

Watch it all arrive

One dashboard, every shipment, zero status meetings. When a widget runs late, you'll know before your line does — with alternatives already queued.
