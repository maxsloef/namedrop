002 / Capabilities

Engineered for
operations teams

— 01

Parametric Configurator

Define widget geometry, tolerances, materials, and finishes once. Acme validates every configuration against live supplier capability data — no more ordering parts nobody can machine.

— 02

Multi-Supplier Ordering

Split a single bill of widgets across qualified suppliers by capacity, lead time, and cost. One checkout, one contract, one source of truth for the whole order book.

— 03

Live Production Tracking

Every widget carries a live status — queued, in production, quality hold, in transit. Drill from a plant-level view down to an individual serial number in two clicks.

— 04

Exception Forecasting

Acme flags at-risk orders an average of eleven days before the missed date, using supplier throughput signals — early enough to re-route, expedite, or renegotiate.

— 05

Spec Version Control

Revision B supersedes revision A everywhere, automatically. Suppliers always build to the current drawing; your ERP always receives the current price.

— 06

ERP & MES Sync

Native connectors for the systems you already run. Purchase orders, receipts, and inventory levels reconcile hourly — no swivel-chair data entry, no drift.
