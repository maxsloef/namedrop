01 / PLATFORM

One system of record for every widget you'll ever order.

Replace spreadsheets, email threads, and tribal knowledge with a single deterministic pipeline — from specification to dock receipt.

CAP—01

Parametric Configuration

Define geometry, alloy, coating, and tolerance class through a constrained specification engine. Invalid combinations are rejected before they cost you a production run.

CAP—02

Fabricator Routing

Orders are matched against 240+ audited fabricators by capability, capacity, and cost. Acme splits multi-line orders automatically and re-routes around disruptions.

CAP—03

Live Telemetry

Every batch reports machine, inspection, and transit events to a unified timeline. Your ERP stays in sync through a streaming API — no more status-chasing calls.

CMM inspection data is captured at the source and validated against your spec before shipment. Non-conforming units never leave the fabricator's floor.

Predictive Lead Times

Quoted dates are computed from live fabricator load, material availability, and lane performance — then held to within a 2% variance band, contractually.

CAP—06

Consolidated Billing

One invoice per period across every fabricator, freight leg, and expedite. Cost centers and GL codes are applied automatically at order time.

03 / PROCESS

From specification to dock in four deterministic steps.

STEP / 01

Specify

Build the widget in the configurator or push a CAD-neutral spec through the API. Validation runs instantly against fabricator capability matrices.

STEP / 02

Route

Acme's routing engine awards the order to the optimal fabricator — or splits it across several — based on price, load, and historical quality.

STEP / 03

Verify

Dimensional data streams in from the shop floor. Anything outside your tolerance band is flagged, quarantined, and remade at no cost to you.

STEP / 04

Receive

Track freight to the minute, receive against your PO automatically, and feed consumption data back into reorder triggers.
