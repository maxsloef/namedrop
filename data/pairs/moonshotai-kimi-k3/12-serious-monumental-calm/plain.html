01 — The Platform

Three disciplines. One system of record.

Spreadsheets and email threads were never built for custom manufacturing. Acme replaces them with a single, durable source of truth.

I.

Configure

Define widgets to the thousandth. Materials, tolerances, finishes, and compliance requirements are captured in structured specifications — validated instantly against what your suppliers can actually build.

II.

Order

Turn approved specifications into purchase orders in one motion. Pricing, lead times, and capacity are negotiated against live supplier data — not last quarter's spreadsheet.

III.

Track

Every widget carries a ledger. Follow units through machining, finishing, inspection, and freight — with exceptions surfaced the hour they happen, not the week after.

02 — The Process

From drawing to dock door, without the noise.

01

Specify

Your engineers define the widget once. Acme locks the revision and distributes it to every party who touches the part.

02

Source

Matched suppliers quote against the live spec. You compare capacity, price, and history side by side.

03

Produce

Milestones report themselves. Deviations in tolerance or schedule trigger review before they compound.

04

Receive

Inspection records travel with the shipment. Arrival closes the loop — and feeds the next forecast.
