01The Platform

One system. Three disciplines. Zero ambiguity.

A

Configure

Define widget geometry, material grade, tolerances, and finish in a structured specification builder. Every parameter is validated against supplier capability before a quote is ever requested — so unbuildable parts never enter your pipeline.

B

Order

Issue RFQs to your approved supplier network, compare bids on normalized terms, and convert awards to purchase orders in a single action. Pricing, lead times, and tooling costs are reconciled automatically against contract rates.

C

Track

Follow every lot through machining, inspection, and freight with milestone-level telemetry. Exceptions surface the moment a tolerance drifts or a shipment slips — not when the line goes down.

02How It Works

From specification to dock in four movements.

1

Specify

Your engineers author a master widget specification once. Acme versions it, governs changes, and distributes it to every relevant supplier.

2

Source

Suppliers bid against the controlled spec. Deviations are flagged line by line, so commercial review takes hours instead of weeks.

3

Produce

In-process inspection data flows directly from the shop floor into Acme. Out-of-tolerance work is quarantined before it ships.

4

Receive

Advance ship notices, certificates of conformance, and freight tracking converge on a single receiving record. Close the loop, keep the audit trail.
