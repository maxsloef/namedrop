Configure

Define widget geometry, material, finish, and tolerance through a constrained configurator. Invalid combinations are impossible by design — the rules of your supply base are encoded, not remembered.

Release orders to qualified suppliers with a single instruction set. Pricing, lead time, and capacity are confirmed at submission — not negotiated afterward in a chain of replies.
