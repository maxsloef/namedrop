How it works

Three steps.
That's the whole thing.

01

Configure

Build your widget spec in a visual configurator — dimensions, materials, tolerances, finishes. It validates as you go, so impossible configs die before they reach a supplier.

02

Order

Get instant pricing and firm lead times from vetted suppliers. One click sends the order — with the spec locked, versioned, and attached forever.

03

Track

Watch every unit move from production line to loading dock on one live board. Delays flag themselves before they become your problem.
